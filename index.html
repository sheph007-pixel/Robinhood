How the projection works
  • Monte Carlo simulation, the same approach Betterment, Vanguard and most robo-advisors use: 5,000 possible markets, month by month.
  • VTI and VXUS each grow at the outlook's yearly return with random ups and downs (about 16% and 17% a year), and they tend to move together (correlation 0.85). Fund fees (0.03% and 0.05%) are taken out.
  • Cash grows at your APY, compounded monthly. Change the APY in Holdings whenever your rate changes.
  • Monthly additions go in at your target mix, and the portfolio is rebalanced to target once a year.
  • Expected is the middle result: half the markets do better, half worse. Poor is the 10th percentile and strong is the 90th, so 8 in 10 outcomes land between them.
  • Historical uses long-run averages (US stocks about 10% a year since 1926, international about 8% since 1970). Moderate and Cautious are closer to what big firms currently forecast for the next decade.
  • Estimates, not promises. Taxes are not included. Not financial advice. Your numbers are saved in this browser only.
