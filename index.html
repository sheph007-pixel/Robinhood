How It Works
  • Each fund's daily buy goes up when that fund is 10% or more below its high, and back to normal as it recovers.
  • High fear (VIX 30+) adds one more step, up to 2× normal, until the VIX is back under 25.
  • Since 1950, money invested 20–30% below the high grew a median 44% over 3 years, versus 36% on an average day.
  • Since 1990, with the S&P 10–30% below its high and the VIX at 30+, the next year's median return was 21–36%, versus −1% to 17% with a lower VIX.
