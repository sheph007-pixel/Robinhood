How to read this

  • The reserve is a dollar amount, so the stock share rises as the total grows. That is by design: the reserve covers living costs, and everything above it works.
  • Buys never ask you to sell. If one fund is over its share, new money goes to the other until they line up.
  • Expected is the middle outcome. Poor and strong are the 1 in 10 results on each side, from 4,000 simulated markets with swings the size stocks have had historically. A crash like 2008 can drop below the poor line for a while.
  • History 8.7% is what a 70/30 VTI and VXUS mix earned a year from June 2001 to November 2026, dividends included. Projections are estimates, not promises. Taxes are not included.
